A note on craft.

Atelier · MMXXVI · § I — On the page

Software has a habit of asking for attention it has not earned. It blinks, pulses, slides in from the corner, suggests, nudges, congratulates. The result is a class of tools that appear busy and feel exhausting.

We started Atelier because we wanted to spend our days inside something quieter. The question we ask of every surface is the same the printer asks of a page: is anything here doing work that the page itself is not already doing?
